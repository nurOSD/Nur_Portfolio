About Me

The Synergy of Human Intuition & Artificial Intelligence in QA.

I am a passionate Quality Assurance Engineer who firmly believes that exceptional software testing requires both human empathy and modern technological power. Real users are humans—which is why my foundation is deeply rooted in Manual Testing. I put myself in the end-user's shoes to explore workflows, examine usability nuances, and uncover edge defects that automated scripts overlook.

At the same time, I supercharge my manual testing with Artificial Intelligence (AI). By using smart AI prompt engineering and generative testing tools, I rapidly generate comprehensive test matrices, discover obscure boundary conditions, and synthesize thousands of realistic test datasets in minutes.

From crafting meticulous Test Scenarios & Test Cases, conducting Exploratory & Regression Testing, to performing API validation in Postman and managing the complete Bug Life Cycle in Jira, I ensure fast-moving Agile development teams ship bulletproof software with total confidence.
